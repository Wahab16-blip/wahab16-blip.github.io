Who I Am

I'm Wahab Olanipekun, a Nigerian-British computer science student with an entrepreneurial streak and a focus on AI engineering. While most students study AI in theory, I build with it — shipping real applications to the internet.

Over six weeks I completed a self-directed AI engineering curriculum covering Python fundamentals, LLM APIs, RAG pipelines, multi-tool agents, full-stack web apps, cloud databases, and document processing with Claude Vision.

My goal is to build AI-powered tools that are genuinely useful — not demos, not wrappers, but products people actually use. My Legal Document Analyser is already being tested by practising lawyers and judges in Nigeria and the UK.

Legal Document Analyser

Live ↗ GitHub ↗

AI-powered contract analysis for legal professionals — supports PDF, Word documents, and photos of paper contracts. Built specifically for UK and Nigerian legal contexts. Being tested by practising lawyers and judges.

  • Claude Vision extracts text from photos of paper documents
  • 7-section AI analysis: parties, obligations, dates, payments, termination
  • Risk flag detection for 25+ risky legal terms
  • RAG-powered Q&A — ask anything about the document
  • UK vs Nigerian law comparison facts
  • PostgreSQL + CASCADE DELETE + bcrypt auth

Personal Finance Tracker

Full-stack finance tracker built for people with variable income — students, part-time workers, freelancers. AI auto-categorises expenses, tracks income vs spending, and gives personalised advice based on actual financial data.

  • Variable income tracking (shifts, allowances, freelance)
  • Net balance and savings rate dashboard
